A production-ready layered asset architecture and cinematic motion direction for the NOVA next-generation laptop landing page.

```text
NOva/
```

## Production Asset Specs

- **Total Production Assets**: 23 modular layers
- **Resolution**:
  - Background Plates: $2560\text{ px} - 3168\text{ px}$ (Native 2.5K to 3.2K)
  - Isolated Product Models: $1735\text{ px} - 2200\text{ px}$ (Anti-aliased Alpha)
  - Detail Macro Cards: $1792 \times 2400\text{ px}$ (Native 4K)
- **Zero Remnants**: 100% clean of text, UI, watermarks, or artificial crops.
